Custos

A private, single-user productivity assistant

Custos helps one person keep track of what needs doing today and tomorrow. It is a personal project, not a public service: there is no sign-up, and it has exactly one user, the person who built it.

What it does

  • Surfaces tasks from email. Custos reads recent Gmail messages the owner has marked for review and suggests possible tasks. Each suggestion is shown for review, and nothing is added to the task list until the owner approves it.
  • Shows the day’s schedule. Custos reads today’s and tomorrow’s Google Calendar events and shows them next to the day’s plan and in an evening review.
  • Captures notes. The owner can jot down ideas and notes, which Custos sorts for later review.

Google account access

Custos asks Google for read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar. It cannot send, delete, label or change email, and it cannot create, edit or delete calendar events. The app runs on a computer the owner controls and can only be reached from the owner’s private network.

The privacy policy explains how Custos accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data, and how to remove it.
